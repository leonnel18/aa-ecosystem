What has been done, who has graduated per region, and what is still to complete

Are we on track?

Needle = share of plans that should be done by now (planned month reached, not cancelled) that are delivered. On track 80%+ · At risk 50–79% · Behind under 50%. Postponed plans count as not delivered. Participation compares attended with target for delivered plans that have a target and a linked training.

By region

Counts are trainings that started in the selected year, from the CRM Trainings and Applications records. Attended = stage “Attended Training” or later. “Still to graduate” = people who attended but whose stage is not yet “Graduated” in the CRM.

Plan vs actual

Every training plan for the year, matched to the actual training(s) linked to it in Zoho (Training Title Plan on the training). Done = a linked training has ended, or the plan is marked Completed. Overdue = planned month has passed and nothing is linked yet. Attended vs target compares people who attended with the plan’s target participants.
